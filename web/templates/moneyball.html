Baseline blends this season's observed pace with real season-by-season track record (recency-weighted, heavier weight on more of the season being played). Floor and Ceiling are NOT a guessed spread — they come from backtesting every completed player-season in this league's own history: for each one, a baseline was projected from only the seasons before it, then compared to what actually happened. The floor/ceiling shown are the 10th/90th percentile of that real error distribution, bucketed by hitter/pitcher and by projection tier. Players are excluded (not just flagged) below a minimum current-season sample ({{ pw.n_excluded_small_sample }} players) or with no qualifying prior season on record to backtest against ({{ pw.n_excluded_no_track_record }} players).
